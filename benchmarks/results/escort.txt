Escort-invariant instrumentation over the triangle family and the
standard matrix (QUESTIONS.md Q14 addendum 10). A ward is a fresh entry
of an incomplete key strictly below the check site's bound; for each
ward a backward search over exactly-tight edges (dis(u) + w = dis(v) in
f64), traversing only non-complete vertices, looks for a complete key
holding a fresh entry in the SAME D strictly below the ward in the
value order — the escort. escort wards base/rec/iter = wards seen per
site; missing = wards with NO escort (any fire falsifies invariant (E)
as stated; the search accepts a superset of canonical tight chains).
Result: wards fire exactly where incomplete_* fired (star part 1,
triangle stride rows — counts coincide event-for-event), and missing is
ZERO on every family, engine, and site. All previously recorded numbers
(triangle.txt, stale_incomplete.txt, restricted_invariant.txt)
reproduce bit-for-bit with the escort columns stripped.
Build: --features verify-contract,dup-classify (observation-only).
