The base-exit triangle driver (QUESTIONS.md Q14 addendum 8/9): the last
settlement of a cutoff-ending base call carries two out-edges u -> z
(w 1.25) and u -> y (w 2.5) plus z -> y (w 0.25), so the post-pull sweep
plants y's non-canonical label below the already-fixed B'. Counters:
incomplete_* = fresh-or-stale entry of an incomplete vertex below the
bound (exact oracle proxy); stale-inc = same but stored value differs
from the key's current label; settle-inc = direct (H) counter,
settlement events at a non-true label length.
